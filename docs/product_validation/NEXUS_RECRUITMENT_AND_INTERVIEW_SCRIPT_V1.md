# NEXUS Recruitment and Interview Script V1

No fabricated interview results.

## Recruitment channels

- Founder warm intros
- Serious self-directed investor communities (not signal groups)
- Concierge waitlist with screener

## Opening script (summary)

We are validating a Decision Integrity Concierge for serious self-directed crypto investors. We do not place trades for you. We help structure Thesis, Evidence, Decision, Monitor, and Review. Participation is time-bound (6–8 weeks). You remain the final decision-maker.

## Core interview blocks

1. Current decision workflow
2. Where Decisions are lost or rewritten
3. Invalidation habits
4. Tool spend and pain
5. AI trust / overconfidence experiences
6. Process vs luck distinction
7. Willingness to record no-action Decisions
8. Stated WTP (hypothesis only)
9. Hard no-buy thresholds
10. Auto-trading demand (disqualify if mandatory)

## Closing

Explain consent, data minimization, export/deletion, and that results will not be invented or anonymously fabricated.
