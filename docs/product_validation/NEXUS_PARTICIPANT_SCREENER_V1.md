# NEXUS Participant Screener V1

## Launch ICP

Serious self-directed Crypto investors with meaningful capital, high research burden, multiple information tools, consequential monthly portfolio decisions, and no professional research team.

## Inclusion

- Self-directed crypto allocation decisions
- Meaningful capital relative to personal finances
- Weekly research burden acknowledged
- Willing to record Decisions and Outcomes
- Can complete English or Traditional Chinese sessions

## Exclusion

- Seeking automated trading / copy trading / signal mirroring
- Professional desk with full research team (later segment)
- Refuses Outcome Review
- Requests withdrawal permissions, write API keys, or seed phrases
- Pure entertainment / meme-only activity without consequential decisions

## Screen questions (score, do not invent answers)

1. Approximate crypto decision frequency (monthly+)
2. Current tools paid/used
3. Whether they keep decision artifacts today
4. Willingness to write invalidation conditions
5. Interest in Decision Integrity vs signals
6. Any demand for auto-orders (auto-exclude if required)
