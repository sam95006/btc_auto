# NEXUS ICP and Interview Plan V1

## Launch ICP (one)

Serious self-directed crypto investors with meaningful capital, high research burden, and no professional research team.

## Inclusion criteria

- Self-directed crypto allocation decisions
- Meaningful capital relative to personal finances (not entertainment-sized only)
- Weekly research time burden acknowledged
- Willing to record Decisions and Outcomes
- English or Traditional Chinese interview capable

## Exclusion criteria

- Seeking automated order execution or copy trading
- Professional sell-side / prop desk with full research team (later segment)
- Users who refuse Outcome Review
- Pure day-trading scalpers wanting only latency tools
- Anyone requesting custody, withdrawals, or API key trading on public product

## Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

When I face a high-stakes crypto Decision under uncertainty, help me assemble evidence, challenge my thesis, record the Decision, monitor invalidation, and review outcomes so I improve calibration — without handing my capital to a black-box bot.

## Recruitment approach

- Founder network warm intros
- Targeted communities for serious self-directed investors (not signal groups)
- Concierge waitlist with screening form
- No paid panel fabrication; no invented interview results

## 20 interview questions

1. How do you currently decide to enter, hold, or exit a crypto thesis?
2. What artifacts do you keep (notes, screenshots, Telegram, Notion)?
3. Where do Decisions get lost or rewritten after the fact?
4. How do you know when a thesis is invalidated?
5. What research steps feel most repetitive?
6. Which tools do you already pay for, and why?
7. When did an AI answer feel useful vs dangerously confident?
8. How do you separate process quality from outcome luck?
9. What would make you open a Decision record before sizing risk?
10. How often do you revisit past Decisions?
11. What alerts do you trust vs ignore?
12. Mobile vs desktop: where do Decisions actually happen?
13. What would make you stop using a Decision tool in week two?
14. Would you pay monthly for Decision Integrity (not signals)?
15. What price feels “obviously too expensive” for your usage?
16. What must never be automated on your behalf?
17. How important is auditability / replay of past Decisions?
18. Do you want AI challenge by default, or only on request?
19. What evidence sources are non-negotiable?
20. If NEXUS never places orders, is that a deal-breaker or a trust feature?

## Willingness-to-pay test

- Present three conceptual packages as **UNVALIDATED_HYPOTHESIS** only
- Ask relative preference and hard no-buy thresholds
- Probe: pay for Decision loops vs pay for more chat tokens
- No fabricated WTP statistics in this document
