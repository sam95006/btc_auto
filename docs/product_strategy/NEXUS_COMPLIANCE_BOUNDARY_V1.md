# NEXUS Compliance Boundary V1

## Product boundaries (public)

| Allowed posture | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Research | Organize public/user evidence |
| Decision Support | Structure options and challenges |
| Risk Awareness | Surface invalidation and uncertainty |
| User-controlled Record | User owns Decision/Outcome/Review |

## Preserve always

- Read-only public product relative to user exchange accounts
- No client asset custody
- No withdrawal permissions
- No automatic orders
- No public strategy marketplace from Private Core
- No guaranteed returns
- No unsupported personalized buy/sell command productization
- Uncertainty and freshness display
- User remains final decision-maker
- Region-specific legal review required before launch in each market

## Disclaimer limitation

A disclaimer alone does **not** eliminate regulation. Product behavior, marketing claims, and jurisdictional rules determine obligations. Legal review is mandatory; this document is not legal advice.
