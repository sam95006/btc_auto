# NEXUS Private Autonomous Trading Mission V1

Canonical Founder-only mission charter for the Private Core.
Do not duplicate this file.

## Permanent status

- The Founder-only autonomous trading mission remains active.
- Public NEXUS (Crypto Decision Integrity Platform) does **not** replace the autonomous trading mission.
- The Private Core is never sold, licensed, exported, or exposed through a public API.
- Losing trades are not automatically process failures.
- Repeated material process errors must be identified, stored as Lessons, retrieved before later decisions, and prevented.
- AI cannot override hard risk controls.
- Permanent changes require Replay, formal Walk-forward, untouched OOS, Risk Review, and Founder approval.
- Demo and real money remain blocked until formal gates pass.

## Reflection principle

Blind Reflection must remain blind to deterministic answers and agreement targets, but must receive the complete sanitized evidence packet required for independent judgment.

Evidence delivery failure is an implementation defect, not proof that a provider lacks reflection ability.
